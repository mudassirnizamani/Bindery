INTRODUCTION
Kya tumhe pata hai tum asli mein kaun ho aur kya karne ke capable ho? Mujhe yakin hai ki tumhe lagta hoga ki pata hai, lekin sirf kisi cheez ko maan lene se woh sach nahi ban jaati. Denial ek ultimate comfort zone hota hai.
Tension mat lo, tum akelay nahi ho. Har sheher mein, har country mein, duniya bhar mein, crores of log sadkon pe ghoom rahe hote hain — dead-eyed zombies ki tarah. Comfort ke addict, victim mentality ko hug kiye hue, aur apne real potential se bilkul unaware.
Mujhe yeh isliye pata hai, kyunki main aise logon se roz milta hoon, unki kahaniyaan sunta hoon. Aur kyunki main khud bhi ek time pe unmein se hi tha — bilkul tumhari tarah.
Aur mere paas toh ek solid excuse bhi tha.
Life ne mujhe ek bad hand diya tha. Main toot ke paida hua tha, bachpan mein maar khaayi, school mein torture jhela, aur itni baar "nigger" bola gaya ki count bhi bhool gaya.
Hum kabhi itne gareeb the ki welfare pe survive kar rahe the, government housing mein rehte the, aur meri depression mujhe choke karti thi. Life ekdum bottom pe thi, aur future ka forecast bilkul hopeless lagta tha.
Bahut kam log jaante hain bottom ka asli feel kya hota hai — lekin main jaanta hoon. Woh quicksand jaise hota hai — pakadta hai, neeche kheechta hai, aur chhodta nahi. Jab life aisi hoti hai, toh comfort zone ke same choices baar-baar repeat karna asaan lagta hai, chahe woh choices tumhe dheere-dheere maar hi kyu na rahi ho.
Par sach yeh hai ki hum sab habitual, self-limiting choices karte hain. Yeh sunset jaise natural hai, gravity jaise basic. Aisa hi dimaag ka wiring hai. Isi liye motivation ek complete crap hai.
Chahe best pep talk ho ya koi fancy self-help trick — sab temporary fix hi hote hain. Woh tumhara dimaag nahi rewire karte.
Woh tumhari awaaz ko powerful nahi banate.
Woh tumhari life ko genuinely uplift nahi karte.
Motivation kisi ka asli transformation nahi karta.
Mere life ka bad hand sirf mera tha — aur usse fix karna bhi sirf mera kaam tha.
Isliye maine dard dhunda, suffering se pyaar kar liya — aur dheere-dheere apne aap ko duniya ke sabse weak insaan se badalkar ek aise hard man mein transform kiya jo shayad God ne kabhi banaya ho — ya fir main khud hi yeh sochta hoon.
Shayad tumhara childhood mere se kaafi better raha ho, aur aaj bhi tumhari life kaafi decent ho.
Lekin chahe tum kaun ho, tumhare parents kaun the, tum kahan rehte ho, kya kaam karte ho, ya tumhare paas kitne paise hain — most probably tum apne asli potential ka sirf 40% hi use kar rahe ho.
Aur yeh sach mein shameful hai.
Hum sab ke andar itna zyada potential hai — hum usse bahut zyada behtar ban sakte hain.
Kuch saal pehle mujhe Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) mein ek panel discussion ke liye bulaya gaya tha. Main kabhi bhi kisi university ke lecture hall mein student ban kar nahi gaya tha. Mushkil se high school pass kiya tha, lekin us din main country ke sabse prestigious institute mein tha — mental toughness pe discussion ke liye.
Discussion ke beech ek well-known MIT professor ne bola ki hum sabke paas genetic limitations hoti hain. Hard ceilings. Kuch cheezein hoti hain jo hum kabhi achieve nahi kar sakte, chahe hum kitne mentally tough kyu na ho. Jab hum apne genetic ceiling tak pahuch jaate hain, toh mental toughness ka koi role nahi rehta — aisa unka kehna tha.
Room mein jitne log baithe the, sabne uski baat maan li — kyunki woh professor senior, tenured, aur mental toughness pe research karta tha. Uska pura career isi pe based tha. Lekin mere liye yeh sab ek excuse tha — science ka use karke sabko easy way out dena.
Main ab tak chup tha, kyunki mere aas-paas itne intelligent log baithe the, aur mujhe thoda stupid feel ho raha tha. Lekin audience mein kisi ne meri expression notice ki aur directly poochha — "Kya aap agree karte ho?"
Aur jab mujhse koi direct question poochhta hai, main chup nahi rehta.
Main bola, “Kuch cheezein samajhne ke bajaye jeene ki bhi value hoti hai,” phir main us professor ki taraf muda.
“Aapki baat mostly logon ke liye sahi hai, lekin 100% nahi. Hamesha kuch 1% log hote hain jo odds ke against jaane ke liye ready hote hain.”
Phir maine apna experience share kiya — ki koi bhi insaan apni puri personality change kar sakta hai, aur woh achieve kar sakta hai jo so-called experts impossible bolte hain. Lekin uske liye chahiye hota hai dil, willpower, aur ek armor jaisa mind.
Heraclitus — jo 5th century BC mein Persian Empire mein hua ek philosopher tha — ne battlefield ke baare mein sahi bola tha:
"100 aadmiyon mein se 10 ko toh wahan hona hi nahi chahiye, 80 sirf targets hote hain, 9 asli fighters hote hain — aur hum lucky hote hain ki woh hamare saath hote hain. Lekin ek, sirf ek hota hai jo warrior hota hai..."
Jab tum pehli baar saans lete ho, tab se tum marne ke eligible ho jaate ho. Lekin ussi moment se tum great banne ke bhi eligible ban jaate ho. Ab yeh tum par hai ki tum khud ko battle ke liye ready karte ho ya nahi.
Sirf tum apne mind ke master ban sakte ho. Aur isi mastery ki zarurat hoti hai ek bold, powerful life jeene ke liye — woh life jo dusre log impossible maante hain.
Main MIT ke un professors ki tarah koi genius nahi hoon, lekin main woh ek warrior hoon. Aur jo story tum ab padhne ja rahe ho — meri fucked-up life ki story — woh tumhe ek proven path dikhayegi self-mastery ka.
Yeh tumhe empower karegi reality ko face karne ke liye, khud ko accountable rakhne ke liye, pain ko cross karne ke liye, dar se pyaar karne ke liye, failure ko enjoy karne ke liye, aur apna full potential jeene ke liye — jahan tum sach mein samjhoge ki tum ho kaun.
Insaan study, habit aur stories ke through change hote hain. Meri story ke through tum samjhoge ki body aur mind kitna capable hote hain jab unhe push kiya jaye maximum level tak — aur wahan tak kaise pahucha jaata hai.
Kyuki jab tum driven hote ho, toh tumhare saamne chahe racism ho, sexism ho, injuries ho, divorce ho, depression ho, obesity ho, tragedy ho ya poverty — yeh sab sirf fuel ban jaata hai tumhari metamorphosis ke liye.
Jo steps yahan diye gaye hain woh ek evolutionary algorithm jaise hain — jo barriers todta hai, glory se shine karta hai, aur tumhe ek lasting peace deta hai.
Mujhe umeed hai tum ready ho.
Ab time aa gaya hai khud ke against war start karne ka.
