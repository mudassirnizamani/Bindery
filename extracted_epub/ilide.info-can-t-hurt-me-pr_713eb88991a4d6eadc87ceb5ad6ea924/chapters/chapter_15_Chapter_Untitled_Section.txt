CHALLENGE #6
Apni Cookie Jar ka inventory lo. Apna journal dubara kholo. Sab kuch likho. Yaad rakho, ye
sirf tumhare trophy room ka halka-phulka tour nahi hai. Sirf apne achievements mat likhna.
Un life obstacles ko bhi include karo jo tumne paar kiye hain, jaise smoking chhodna,
depression se nikalna, ya stammering ko overcome karna. Un choti failures ko bhi likho jo
pehle nahi hue, lekin doosri ya teesri baar try kar ke tumne complete kiye. Mehsoos karo un
struggles ko, un opponents ko, aur jeet ka ehsaas. Phir kaam pe lag jao.
Har workout se pehle ambitious goals set karo aur un past victories se naye personal bests
tak pahucho. Agar run ya bike ride kar rahe ho, to thoda interval work add karo aur apna
best mile split beat karne ki koshish karo. Ya sirf max heart rate ek minute maintain karo,
phir do minute. Agar ghar par ho, to pull-ups ya push-ups par focus karo. Do minute mein
jitne ho sake karo. Phir apna best todne ki try karo. Jab pain aaye aur goal ke paas rokne
lage, apna haath daalo, ek cookie nikalo, aur use apna fuel banao.
Agar tumhara focus intellectual growth par hai, to apne aap ko harder aur longer study
karne ke liye train karo, ya ek month mein record number of books padhne ka challenge lo.
Tumhari Cookie Jar yahan bhi help karegi. Kyunki agar tumne ye challenge sahi se kiya, to
kisi bhi exercise mein ek point aayega jab pain, boredom, ya self-doubt ayega, aur tumhe
uss moment ko paar karne ke liye push karna padega. Cookie Jar tumhare thought process
ka control lene ka shortcut hai. Use usi tarah! Point ye nahi hai ki tum khud ko hero
mehsoos karao. Ye koi hooray-for-me moment nahi hai. Ye isliye hai taaki tum yaad karo ki
tum kitne badass ho, taaki uss energy se tum ek aur jeet hasil kar sako jab sab kuch mushkil
lage.

karo:
