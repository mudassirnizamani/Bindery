Humne ek khubsurat neighborhood mein Hell dhoondh liya. 1981 mein, Williamsville Buffalo, New York ka sabse desirable real estate spot tha. Green aur friendly jagah, jahan safe streets par chhoti-chhoti sundar homes thi jo model citizens se bhari thi.
