Main zyada buoyant nahi tha. Jab bhi main tairna rokta, thodi der ke liye bhi, main doobne lagta, jisse mera dil panic mein tez dhadakne lagta, aur tension badhne lagti, jo situation aur worse kar deti. Aakhirkar, main swim test pass kar gaya, lekin competent aur comfortable hone mein fark hota hai, aur comfortable se confident tak aur bada gap hota hai. Jab tum zyada logon ki tarah float nahi kar pate, toh water confidence aasani se nahi aata, kabhi kabhi bilkul nahi aata.
Pararescue training mein water confidence 10-week program ka ek hissa hota hai, jisme specific exercises hoti hain jisse test kiya jata hai ki hum paani mein stress mein kitna perform kar sakte hain. Sabse mushkil exercise thi Bobbing. Class ko 5 ke groups mein baanta gaya, shallow pool ke gutter se gutter tak line lagayi, aur hume full gear pehna gaya. Hamare peeth par 80-liter ke twin tanks lage the, jo galvanized steel ke the, aur 16-pound weight belt bhi tha. Hum full load pe the, jo thik tha, lekin is exercise mein hum tanks se saans nahi le sakte the. Hume kaha gaya tha ki hum pool ke 3-foot shallow section se peeche ki taraf chal ke deep end tak (lagbhag 10 feet neeche) jayen, aur wahan position mein aayen. Jab main wahan ja raha tha, mera dimag doubt aur negativity se bhar gaya tha. “Kya kar raha hai yahan? Ye tere liye nahi hai! Tu tair nahi sakta! Tu impostor hai, wo tujhe pakad lenge!”
Time slow ho gaya, seconds minute lag rahe the. Mera diaphragm hawa lungi ki koshish kar raha tha. Theoretically, main janta tha ki relaxation paani ke neeche sabse zaroori hai, lekin main dar ke mare chhoot nahi pa raha tha. Meri jaw tight thi, jaise mere fists. Mera sir dard kar raha tha, panic ko rokne ki koshish mein. Aakhirkar, sab position mein aa gaye aur bobbing shuru hua. Iska matlab tha bottom se surface tak bina fins ka use kiye upar uthna, saans lena, phir doob jana. Pure loaded hone ke bawajood upar aana mushkil tha, lekin pehli saans lene par relief milta tha. Oxygen mujhe relax karta tha, jab tak instructor ne “Switch!” nahi bola. Matlab apne pairon ke fins haathon mein leke ek pull se surface tak aana tha. Floor ko push kar sakte the, lekin kick nahi kar sakte the. Ye 5 minute chala.
Shallow water aur surface blackouts common hote hain water confidence training mein. Ye body pe stress aur oxygen intake limit karne se hota hai. Haathon mein flippers pehne main apna face paani se nikal ke saans lene ki koshish karta, aur beech mein oxygen jaldi jaldi use karta. Jab oxygen jaldi jaldi khatam hoti hai, toh dimaag bandh ho jata hai aur tum blackout kar jate ho. Humare instructors isse “meeting the wizard” kehte the. Clock chal raha tha, aur mere peripheral vision mein stars chamak rahe the, wizard paas aa raha tha.
Maine ye evolution pass kiya, aur jaldi hi arm ya feet se finning aasan ho gaya. Lekin sabse mushkil task jo shuru se end tak raha wo tha bina haathon ke paani mein tairna. Hume apne haath aur chin paani se upar rakhne the, sirf legs ka blender jaisa motion use karte hue, 3 minute tak. Ye time zyada nahi lagta, aur class ke zyada log ke liye aasan tha. Mere liye ye nearly impossible tha. Mera chin baar baar paani mein girta, jisse time triple zero se start hota. Mere aas paas classmates itne comfortable the ki unke legs bilkul slow chal rahe the, aur mere legs top speed pe chal rahe the, tab bhi main un white boys jitna high tair nahi pa raha tha, jo lag rahe the jaise gravity ko challenge kar rahe ho.

Dheere-dheere dar badh ke gussa ban gaya apne classmates pe, jinhe main asaan samajhta tha, aur ye sab meri bachpan ki yaadein bhi lekar aata tha.
