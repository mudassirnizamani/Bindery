CHAPTER  TWO
TRUTH HURTS
Wilmoth Irving ek nayi shuruaat thi. Jab tak unki mulakaat meri mom se nahi hui thi aur unhone unka phone number nahi maanga tha, tab tak meri zindagi sirf dard aur struggle se bhari thi. Jab paisa tha tab bhi hamari life sirf trauma se bhari hoti thi. Aur jab hum mere father se free hue, tab bhi hum apne hi PTSD-level ke dard aur gareebi mein doob gaye.
Phir fourth grade mein meri mom ki mulakaat Wilmoth se hui — ek successful carpenter aur general contractor, jo Indianapolis se tha. Mom ko uski easy smile aur relaxed nature pasand aayi. Usme koi violence nahi tha. Uski wajah se humein pehli baar lagaa jaise hum thoda sa chain le sakte hain. Uske hone se aisa laga jaise finally koi support mil raha ho, jaise kuch accha ho raha ho hamare saath.
Jab bhi mom Wilmoth ke saath hoti thi, wo khul ke hasti thi. Uski smile bright aur real hoti thi. Wo zyada seedhi chalti thi, confident dikhti thi. Wilmoth ne use dobara khud pe garv feel karna sikhaya, usse phir se khud ko beautiful mehsoos karvaya. Mere liye, Wilmoth ek healthy father figure jaise tha. Usne mujhe kabhi zyada pamper nahi kiya, na kabhi fake I love you-type baatein boli, lekin wo hamesha available tha.
Basketball mera bachpan se obsession tha, aur Johnny Nichols mera best friend tha jiske saath maine basketball ka connection banaya. Wilmoth ko bhi game aata tha. Hum aksar courts pe jaate, wo mujhe moves sikhata, defense improve karvata, jump shot practice karvata. Hum teeno birthdays aur holidays milke celebrate karte. Eighth grade ke summer se pehle, usne mom ke saamne ghutne tek kar proposal diya.
Wilmoth Indianapolis mein rehta tha, aur plan tha ki next summer hum uske saath shift ho jaayenge. Wo Trunnis ki tarah ameer nahi tha, lekin achha kama leta tha. Humein ek baar phir city life milne wali thi. Phir 1989 mein, Christmas ke ek din baad sab kuch ruk gaya.
Hum abhi tak full-time uske saath shift nahi hue the, lekin usne Christmas humare saath Brazil (Indiana) mein bitaya. Agle din uska ek men’s league basketball match tha, aur usne mujhe sub karne ke liye invite kiya tha. Main itna excited tha ki do din pehle hi bag pack kar liya tha. Lekin uss morning usne bola, “Is baar main tumhe yahin rakh raha hoon, Little David.”
Main udaas ho gaya, lekin maine haan mein sir hila diya. Mom aur maine porch se usse wave karte dekha jaise hamesha karta tha. Wo car mein baitha aur chala gaya.
Yeh uska last goodbye tha.
Us raat usne game khela, aur fir apne ghar laut gaya — jise wo “house with the white lions” ke naam se describe karta tha. Jaise hi usne garage mein car park ki, kuch log jo ghanto se chhup kar wait kar rahe the, uske piche se aaye aur point-blank uske chest mein 5 goliyaan maar di. Jab wo garage floor pe gir gaya, tab shooter ne uske sir mein ek aur goli maar ke uska kaam khatam kar diya.
Wilmoth ke father kuch blocks door rehte the. Agle din jab wo uske garage ke paas se guzre aur dekha door ab tak open hai, unhe kuch galat laga. Wo andar gaye aur apne bete ke dead body ke paas ro diye.
Wilmoth sirf 43 years ka tha.
Main abhi bhi nani ke ghar tha jab Wilmoth ki mom ne call kiya. Nani ne mujhe bulaya aur news di. Main turant mom ke baare mein sochne laga. Wilmoth uska savior tha. Usne dobara jeena start kiya tha, achhe cheezon par bharosa karna start kiya tha. Ab kya hoga uska?
Main gusse se bhadak gaya. Fridge pe punch maara aur us mein dent aa gaya.
Hum ghar gaye jahan mom already tension mein thi, kyunki Wilmoth ka koi contact nahi aaya tha. Usne uske ghar call kiya aur jab phone pe ek detective ne uthaya, toh wo confused ho gayi. Nani ne phone leke usse bitha diya.
Pehle mom ko bharosa hi nahi hua. Use laga Wilmoth koi prank khel raha hai. Lekin phir use yaad aaya ki do mahine pehle usse goli lagi thi. Tab Wilmoth ne kaha tha ki wo goli uske liye nahi thi. Usne us baat ko ignore kar diya tha. Us moment tak, use kabhi nahi laga tha ki Wilmoth ki koi secret street life ho sakti hai. Police bhi kabhi clear reason nahi dhoond payi. Bas yeh guess tha ki koi shady ya drug deal mein kuch gadbad hui thi.
Mom tab bhi denial mein thi jab usne bag pack kiya, lekin usne ek dress funeral ke liye rakh li.
Jab hum Wilmoth ke ghar pahunche, pura house police ke yellow tape se ghera hua tha, jaise koi scary Christmas gift. Ye koi prank nahi tha. Mom ne car park ki, tape ke neeche se guzri, aur main uske piche-piche front door tak gaya. Main left side dekh raha tha, jahan Wilmoth ko maara gaya tha. Garage floor par uska fresh blood abhi bhi jama hua tha.
Main sirf 14 saal ka tha, ek active crime scene mein ghoom raha tha, lekin kisi ko fark hi nahi pada. Na mom ko, na Wilmoth ke family members ko, na hi police ko. Jaise main wahan hoon ya nahi, koi matter hi nahi karta tha.
Jitna messed up lagta hai sunne mein, us raat police ne mom ko Wilmoth ke ghar rukne diya. Wo akeli na rahe isliye usne apne brother-in-law ko bulaya, jiske paas do guns the, just in case killers wapas aayein. Main Wilmoth ki sister ke ghar gaya, jo ek dark aur creepy house tha kuch miles door. Raat bhar wahan akela tha. TV purana cabinet type tha, 13 channel waala. Sirf 3 channels clear aate the, aur main local news laga ke baitha raha.
Wahi ek hi news loop chalti rahi: main aur mom police tape ke neeche jhuk ke ja rahe hain, aur Wilmoth ka body gurney pe le ja rahe hain, sheet se dhaka hua. Jaise kisi horror movie ka repeat scene chal raha ho. Main bas wahi dekhte raha, baar-baar. Har baar dekhne par andar ka darkness aur ghera ho jaata tha. Mera dimag ek broken record jaise ho gaya tha jo bar-bar ek hi jagah atak raha tha.
Past already dark tha, ab jo thoda hope tha future ka, wo bhi gayab ho gaya. Jaise ek baar fir wahi purana hell wapas aa gaya ho. Har baar woh footage dekhkar, dar aur badhta gaya, phir bhi main dekhna band nahi kar paaya.
Wilmoth ka funeral hone ke kuch din baad, aur naye saal ke turant baad, main wapas school bus mein chadha Brazil, Indiana mein. Abhi bhi grief mein tha, confused bhi, kyunki decide nahi hua tha ki hum yahin rahenge ya phir Indianapolis move karenge jaise pehle plan tha. Mom shock mein thi. Wilmoth ke death pe ab tak wo royi bhi nahi thi. Uski aankhon mein emotions dead ho chuke the, jaise zindagi bhar ka dard ek hi jagah jama ho gaya ho aur wo us mein khud kho gayi ho. Us se connect kar pana impossible tha.
Tab tak school wapas start ho gaya tha, toh main bhi normalcy ka kuch tukda pakadne ki koshish kar raha tha.
Lekin aasaan nahi tha. Bus mein first day baithte hi ek purani memory wapas aa gayi — ek horror scene jo maine bury kar diya tha. Pichle saal, ek morning main usual seat pe tha, jab bus school ke bahar rukhi aur ek chhota ladka cookies leke hamari bus ki taraf bhaag ke aaya. Uska driver ne usse nahi dekha. Bus ne sudden move kiya.
Uski maa ka chehra dekhte hi pata chal gaya kuch galat hone wala hai. Agle second, blood ne meri window ko red kar diya. Uski maa ne aisi cheekh maari jaise koi injured jaanwar. Usne literally apne baal jhad liye, roots se. Sirens ke awaz chilla rahe the, aur sab kuch chaos tha. Ladka sirf 6 saal ka tha. Wo cookies driver ke liye gift leke aaya tha.
Sab ko bus se utarne ko kaha gaya. Jab main tragedy ke paas se guzra, kuch ajeeb attraction tha andhere ki taraf. Maine neeche jhaank ke dekha — uska head paper jaise flat ho chuka tha. Blood aur brain oil ki tarah ground pe fail gaya tha.
Ek saal tak maine wo scene bhool diya tha. Lekin Wilmoth ke death ne us memory ko jaga diya. Ab sirf wahi sochta tha. Ab kuch bhi matter nahi karta tha. Duniya sirf dard aur tragedy se bhari thi, aur wo itna bhar chuka tha ki ab mujhe bhi nigalne wala tha.
Main bed pe sona chhod diya. Mom bhi arm chair pe soti thi TV ya book leke. Main pehle bed try karta tha, lekin hamesha floor pe fetal position mein uthta tha. Phir finally floor pe hi sona start kar diya. Shayad isliye kyunki agar neeche ka comfort mil gaya, toh ab aur neeche girne ko kuch bacha hi nahi.
Hum dono — main aur meri mom — desperately ek nayi shuruaat chahte the, isliye Wilmoth ke bina bhi humne Indianapolis shift hone ka decision liya. Mom ne mujhe Cathedral High School ke entry exam ke liye register kiya, jo ek private college prep school tha city ke beech mein. Aur jaise hamesha, maine exam mein cheating ki — wo bhi ek smart ladke se.
Jab acceptance letter aur class schedule summer ke end mein aaya, toh pata chala ki mujhe full AP classes mil gayi hain! Maine somehow cheating aur copying karke first year nikal liya. Basketball team mein bhi selection ho gaya — aur wo team state ki best freshman teams mein se ek thi. Main point guard bana, jo mere liye confidence boost tha. Lekin andar se mujhe pata tha ki main ek academic fraud hoon. Aur school ki fees bhi meri mom afford nahi kar pa rahi thi, toh ek saal baad hi usne mujhe Cathedral se nikal liya.
Sophomore year main North Central High School mein shift hua — ek public school jisme 4,000 students the aur zyada tar black community se. Pehle din hi main ek preppy white boy jaisa dikhta tha — tight jeans, collared shirt andar takka hua, aur braided belt. Agar main basketball nahi khelta, toh us din mujhe pura school troll kar deta.
Pura sophomore year cool banne ke chakkar mein nikal gaya. Maine apna look change kiya — hip-hop culture se inspire hone laga, aur gang banger type students ke saath ghoomne laga. School bunk karna bhi common ho gaya. Ek din mom ghar aayi aur dekha main dining table ke paas baitha hoon, dus “thugs” ke saath. Aur wo jhoot nahi tha. Kuch hi hafton mein, mom ne fir se sab pack kiya aur hum wapas Brazil, Indiana chale gaye.
Main Northview High School mein usi week admit hua jab basketball tryouts ho rahe the. Lunch time par school pahuncha jab cafeteria full tha. School mein 1,200 students the, aur unmein sirf 5 black kids. Jab maine pehle school chhoda tha, tab tak main bhi unke jaisa hi lagta tha — lekin ab nahi.
Us din main loose jeans pehne tha — jo mere size se 5 size bade the — sagged down low, ek oversized Chicago Bulls jacket, aur ek backward tilted cap ke saath school mein enter kiya. Sabki nazar mujh par thi — students, teachers, staff — sab mujhe aise dekh rahe the jaise main koi alien hoon. Shayad unmein se kai logon ne pehli baar ek real thug-style black ladka dekha tha. Jaise hi main andar gaya, pura mahaul ruk gaya. Music jaise scratch ho gaya ho. Jaise hip-hop ka raw beat school ke andar ghoom gaya ho. Sab notice kar rahe the, lekin sabko wo pasand nahi aaya.
Main aise act kar raha tha jaise mujhe fark hi nahi padta, lekin sach yeh tha ki andar se main insecure tha. Buffalo ka life ek hellfire tha. Brazil ke early years mein mujhe PTSD mil chuka tha. Phir bhi, Indianapolis mein mujhe ek break mila tha — naye doston ke saath, nayi style, thoda confidence. Ab wapas aake main bahar se toh naya lag raha tha, lekin andar se wahi purana confused kid tha, jiske paas kuch bhi solid base nahi tha.
Basketball tryouts ne meri bachi-kuchi confidence bhi le li.
Jab main gym pahuncha, toh unhone mujhe regular gym clothes pehnne nahi diye — balki puri uniform pehnwai. Us waqt style tha baggy shorts ka — jaisa Fab Five ke Chris Webber aur Jalen Rose famous banane wale the. Lekin Brazil ke coaches abhi bhi purani world mein the. Unhone mujhe super tight, Larry Bird-era basketball shorts pehnaye — jo literally uncomfortable the. Meri thighs squeeze ho rahi thi, aur lag raha tha jaise main kisi aur time zone mein aa gaya hoon. Larry Legend unka hero tha — aur poore Indiana ka bhi. Uski beti bhi mere school mein padhti thi — hum friends bhi the — lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki main uski tarah dress karna chahta tha!
Phir aayi meri court attitude wali problem. Indianapolis mein toh coaches trash talk ko allow karte the. Agar main kisi ke upar shot maarta tha, toh uski girlfriend ya mom tak ki baat kar deta. Main research karke expert ban gaya tha apne trash talk mein. Draymond Green jaisa.
Lekin Brazil mein yeh allowed nahi tha. Jaise hi tryouts shuru hue, maine kaafi moves dikhaye, kuch logon ko cross karke embarrass bhi kiya, aur sabke saamne show bhi kiya. Coaches ko meri attitude se problem ho gayi. Ironically, unka hero Larry Bird bhi all-time great trash talker tha, lekin unhone mujhe ignore kiya. Jaldi hi unhone ball mere haath se le liya aur mujhe front court mein shift kar diya — ek position jo maine kabhi khela bhi nahi tha. Main uncomfortable tha, aur meri game bhi waisi hi ho gayi. Confidence wahi khatam ho gaya.
Us hafte ka mera eklauta positive moment tha Johnny Nichols ke saath fir se connection banana. Hum door rehkar bhi close the, aur jaise hi main wapas aaya, hamare intense one-on-one basketball matches wapas full swing mein shuru ho gaye. Johnny thoda undersized tha, lekin talent mein strong tha — tryouts ke time wo court pe best players mein se ek tha. Shots drain kar raha tha, open teammates dekh raha tha, aur court pe control mein tha. Jab uska varsity selection hua, toh surprise nahi hua, lekin jab mujhe sirf JV mein liya gaya, toh hum dono shocked the.
Mujhe sirf basketball tryouts ke result se hurt nahi hua — wo sirf ek aur nishani thi ek deeper problem ki jo main feel kar raha tha. Brazil, Indiana pehle jaise hi dikh raha tha, lekin ab sab kuch alag lag raha tha. Grade school time pe academics tough the, lekin tab tak mujhe racism feel nahi hua tha — even though hum kuch hi black families mein se ek the. Lekin ab jab main teenager tha, har jagah mujhe racism mehsoos hone laga. Aur yeh isliye nahi tha ki main overly sensitive ho gaya tha. Truth yeh tha ki racism pehle se hi tha — bas ab main usse ignore nahi kar pa raha tha.
Brazil wapas aane ke kuch time baad main aur mere cousin Damien ek party mein gaye, jo city se kaafi door countryside mein thi. Hum curfew cross kar gaye aur poori raat wahin rahe. Subah hone par humne dadi ko phone kiya ride ke liye.
“Excuse me?” unhone kaha. “Tumne meri baat nahi maani, toh ab chal ke aao.”
Samajh gaya.
Wo 10 miles door rehti thi, ek lambi country road ke end par. Lekin hum mazak karte hue chalte rahe. Damien Indianapolis se tha, aur hum dono sagging jeans aur oversized Starter jackets mein the — jo Brazil ke country roads pe common dress nahi thi.
7 miles chalne ke baad ek purana pick-up truck humari taraf aata dikha. Hum road ke side ho gaye taki truck nikal jaye. Lekin wo dheere ho gaya, aur jab wo humare paas se nikla, toh andar 2 teenagers the aur ek piche truck bed mein khada tha. Ek ne khidki se chillaya: “Niggers!”
Hum react nahi kiye. Bas sir jhuka ke normal pace mein chalna continue rakha. Tabhi peeche se brakes chille, gravel uda, aur truck ruk gaya. Tab maine dekha ek redneck-looking ladka truck se utar kar pistol leke meri taraf aa raha tha.
“Where the fuck you from, and why the fuck you here in this fucking town?!”
Damien dheere se thoda door hat gaya. Main seedha us aadmi ki aankhon mein dekh raha tha. Mere skin pe chills aa gaye, lekin maine bhaagna ya darna choose nahi kiya. Wo mere sirf 2 feet door aaya. Bas kuch seconds baad wo wapas truck mein chadha aur chale gaye. Yeh pehli baar nahi tha jab mujhe us word ka samna karna pada. Usse pehle bhi ek din main Johnny aur kuch girls ke saath Pizza Hut mein baitha tha. Unmein ek brunette ladki thi Pam — jo mujhe pasand thi, aur wo bhi mujhe like karti thi. Lekin humne kabhi kuch start nahi kiya tha. Bas ek innocent friendship thi. Jab uske dad usse pick karne aaye, unhone hume ek saath dekh liya. Pam ka chehra turant pale ho gaya.
Uska father full restaurant mein ghus aaya, sabki nazar us par thi. Usne mujhse ek word bhi nahi bola — sirf Pam ki aankhon mein dekha aur bola:
“I don’t want to ever see you sitting with this nigger again.”
Pam turant nikal gayi, sharam se uska chehra red tha. Main wahin baitha reh gaya, floor ko ghoorta hua — frozen. Yeh meri life ka sabse humiliating moment tha. Gun incident se bhi zyada hurtful. Kyunki yeh public mein hua tha, aur yeh word kisi immature ladke ne nahi, ek grown man ne kaha tha. Main samajh hi nahi paaya ki kisi ke andar itni nafrat kyun hoti hai. Aur agar uske andar hai, toh kya Brazil ke aur log bhi mujhe aise hi dekhte hain jab main road pe walk karta hoon?
Yeh wo type ka sawal tha jiska jawab tum lena hi nahi chahte.
Main second year mein tha Brazil, Indiana ke high school mein, aur uss saal mera rule simple tha — “Agar wo mujhe dekhenge hi nahi, toh bulayenge bhi nahi.” Main hamesha back row mein chup-chaap baithta, chair mein dhans ke rehta, aur har class bas side mein nikal deta. Us saal school mein ek foreign language lena compulsory tha. Yeh sunke mujhe hasi aayi — isliye nahi ki language ki value nahi samajhta tha, balki isliye ki English hi mushkil thi, Spanish toh door ki baat thi.
8 saal tak cheat karke pass hota raha, lekin asli mein kuch bhi nahi seekha. Lagta tha system ko fool bana raha hoon, lekin asal mein khud ko hi fool bana raha tha.
Ek din Spanish class mein jaake apna workbook uthaya — har student ka apna assigned workbook hota tha, aur mere wale pe upar kone mein mera naam likha tha. Main apni seat pe dhans gaya, jaise hamesha karta tha, acting ki jaise sun raha hoon. Lekin jab page open kiya, sab kuch ekdum silent lagne laga.
Teacher bol rahi thi, lekin mujhe kuch sunayi nahi diya. Meri nazar us page pe ruk gayi jahan kisi ne mere liye ek drawing banayi thi — mere neck mein noose, ek childish “hangman” style mein. Neeche likha tha:
"Niger we’re gonna kill you!"
Spelling galat thi, lekin mujhe kya pata? Main khud mushkil se spell karta tha. Message bilkul clear tha.
Mere andar ka gussa tufan ki tarah uth raha tha. Kaun tha jo mujhe maarna chahta tha sirf meri skin color ke liye? Mujhe laga, “Main yahan wapas aane ke liye nahi tha. Mujhe Brazil wapas nahi aana chahiye tha!”
Teacher ab bhi lecture mein busy thi jab main ekdum se uth gaya. Usne mera naam liya, lekin main sunna nahi chahta tha. Main seedha principal ke office gaya, bina rukhe, aur notebook uske desk pe phenka.

Us waqt principal the
Kirk Freeman
. Aaj bhi unhe yaad hai ki mere aankhon mein aansu the. Unhe pata tha ki Brazil jaisi jagah mein aisa hona koi nayi baat nahi thi. Southern Indiana hamesha se racist logon se bhara hua tha. 4 saal baad, 1995 mein, Ku Klux Klan ne Independence Day ke din Brazil ke main road pe full uniform mein march bhi kiya tha. Center Point jaise towns — jo sirf 15 minute door the — wahan se bhi students aate the, jo mere school mein mere peeche baithte aur har din racist jokes maarke enjoy karte.
Mujhe koi badi investigation ki umeed nahi thi. Sirf thodi empathy chahiye thi. Principal Freeman ne drawing aur message ko dekha, fir meri taraf dekha aur bola:
“David, yeh toh sirf ignorance hai. Dekho, unhe nigger ka spelling bhi nahi aata.”
Meri jaan ka khatra tha, aur unke paas bas itna hi kehne ko tha.
Main jab office se bahar nikla, jo tanhaayi maine mehsoos ki — wo kabhi bhool nahi sakta. School ke corridors mein itni nafrat thi ki kisi anjaan ne sirf meri skin color ke basis pe mujhe maarne ki dhamki de di thi. Sabse bada dar yeh tha ki mujhe pata bhi nahi tha ki mera dushman kaun tha. Kya history class ka koi redneck tha? Ya koi aisa banda jo dikhane ke liye dost tha, lekin asli mein mujhe pasand nahi karta tha?
Sadak pe gun dekhna ya kisi racist baap ka saamna karna alag baat thi — wo sab at least seedha tha, sach tha. Lekin yeh hidden nafrat, jo dikhayi nahi deti thi, school ki har wall pe invisible ink se likhi hui lagti thi. Aur yeh soch har din mujhe tod rahi thi.
Mere paas dost the, lekin sab white. Phir bhi main har jagah bas ek cheez mehsoos karta tha —
“being the only.”
America mein zyadaatar minorities, women, aur gay log ek specific loneliness ka pressure samajhte hain — wo feeling jab aap kisi room mein jaate ho aur sirf aap hi apne jaise ho. White males mein se bahut kam log samajh paate hain ki ye kitna exhausting ho sakta hai. Kaash wo samajh paate, toh unhe yeh pata chalta ki kuch din aise hote hain jab bas ghar mein rehna aur chup rehna best lagta hai — kyunki bahar nikalna matlab khud ko expose karna hota hai ek aise duniya ke saamne jo tumhe har angle se judge karti hai. Shayad har waqt aisa nahi ho raha hota, lekin lagta zaroor hai. Aur wahi feeling khud mein ek mental torture ban jaati hai.
Brazil mein main har jagah “only” tha. Lunch time mein apne doston ke saath table pe, har class mein, aur basketball gym mein bhi.
Us saal ke end tak main 16 ka ho gaya, aur mere grandfather ne mujhe ek purani si, brown Chevy Citation gift ki. School ke pehle hi kuch dino mein kisi ne mere car ke driver side door pe spray paint se “nigger” likh diya — aur iss baar spelling bhi correct thi. Principal Freeman fir se kuch bol nahi paaye. Mere andar jo gussa tha us din, use describe nahi kar sakta — lekin wo bahar nahi nikla. Wo mujhe andar hi todta raha. Mujhe samajh nahi aata tha itni emotion ka kya karoon.
Kya mujhe sabse ladna chahiye? Main pehle hi 3 baar school se fighting ke liye suspend ho chuka tha, aur ab toh main almost numb tha. Toh maine apne aap ko aur bhi zyada alag kar diya, aur black nationalism ki taraf khinch gaya. Malcolm X mere favorite ban gaye. Main school se ghar aake unki speeches ka ek hi video roz dekhta tha. Unka system ke against gussa, unka words ka power — wo mujhe comfort deta tha. Haan, unke political ya economic views mujhe samajh nahi aate the, lekin unka anger relatable tha — ek system ke khilaaf jo sirf white logon ke liye bana tha. Main khud bhi ek ajeeb hate aur frustration mein jee raha tha. Lekin mujhme Nation of Islam jaise discipline nahi tha.
Junior year tak main intentionally logon ko provoke karne laga. Main wohi stereotype ban gaya jise racist log sabse zyada hate karte hain. Roz pants ko hips se neeche pehenta tha, apne car ke stereo ko home speakers se connect karke itna loud karta tha ki Brazil ki sadkein hil jaati thi. Snoop Dogg ke “Gin and Juice” pe car blast hoti thi, steering wheel pe 3 shag covers laga diye the, aur rearview mirror se fuzzy dice latka diye the. Roz subah mirror ke saamne khada hota aur sochta ki aaj kaise racism se khelna hai.
Main wild hairstyles bhi try karta. Ek baar maine apne baal almost pura shave kar diya, bas ek thin line side mein chhod ke. Main popular tha, "cool black kid" mana jaata tha. Lekin agar koi deeply observe karta, toh wo dekh sakta tha ki main black culture ko celebrate nahi kar raha tha — main bas lost tha. Meri har harkat ka ek hi purpose tha — un logon ko chidhana jo mujhe sabse zyada hate karte the. Kyunki har kisi ka opinion mere liye important tha — aur yeh ek bahut shallow tareeka tha jeene ka.
Main andar se pain se bhara tha, koi real purpose nahi tha, aur door se dekhne wale soch rahe hote ki yeh ladka toh barbaadi ki taraf jaa raha hai. Lekin sach yeh tha ki main abhi bhi hope hold kiye hua tha. Mere paas ek last dream tha —
Air Force join karna.

Civil Air Patrol
join kiya — jo Air Force ka civilian training program hota hai. Hum weekly milte, formation mein march karte, aur Air Force ke different roles ke baare mein seekhte the.

PJOC (Pararescue Jump Orientation Course).
Aur jaise hamesha, main wahan bhi “the only” tha.
