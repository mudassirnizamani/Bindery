CHALLENGE #3
Calloused mind banane ka pehla step hai apne comfort zone se regular basis pe bahar nikalna. Apna journal nikaalo aur likho wo saari cheezein jo tumhe pasand nahi ya jo tumhe uncomfortable feel karati hain — khaaskar wo jo tumhare liye achhi hain.
Ab unme se koi ek kaam karo, aur phir se karo.
Aage ke pages me main tumse kahunga ki tum uss process ko thoda mirror karo, par iska matlab yeh nahi ki tum apni life ka impossible task jaldi complete karo. Yeh journey chhoti-chhoti progress karne ki hai, jo sustainable ho. Matlab har din koi aisi chhoti cheez karo jo tumhe thoda uncomfortable banaye — jaise apna bed banana, bartan dhona, kapde iron karna, ya subah jaldi uth ke 2 mile daud lagana.
Jab yeh aasan lage, toh 5 mile, phir 10 mile karo. Agar tum pehle se ye sab karte ho, toh apni life me koi aisi cheez dhoondo jo tum nahi karte ya jisme behtari ki zarurat ho. Hum aksar apni strengths par focus karte hain, par ab apni weaknesses ko apni strength banane ka time hai.
Jo bhi kaam tumhe uncomfortable kare, chhota ya bada, karte raho. Jitna zyada tum discomfort mehsoos karoge, utna hi zyada strong banoge. Stressful situations me tum apne aap se ek positive, can-do baat kar paoge.
Aur haan, apni ek photo ya video lo jab tum discomfort zone me ho, use social media pe post karo, batao tum kya kar rahe ho aur kyun. Hashtags #discomfortzone #pathofmostresistance #canthurtme #impossibletask lagana mat bhoolna.
