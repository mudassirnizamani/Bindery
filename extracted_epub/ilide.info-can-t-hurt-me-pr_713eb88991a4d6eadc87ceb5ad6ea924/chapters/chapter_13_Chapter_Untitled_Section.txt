CHALLENGE #5
Yeh waqt hai apni soch ko visualize karne ka! Zyada tar log har ghante 2,000 se 3,000
sochte hain, par aksar woh bekaar ki baaton par atak jaate hain jo badal nahi sakte. Iske
bajaye, un cheezon par dhyaan do jo tum badal sakte ho. Koi bhi rukawat ya naya goal chun
lo, aur socho ki tum kaise usse paar paoge ya haasil karoge.
Meri aadat hai ki main har mushkil kaam shuru karne se pehle apni success ka ek tasveer
banata hoon — ki main jeet raha hoon, kaisa feel ho raha hai. Roz ye sochta hoon, aur woh
feeling mujhe aage badhne mein madad karti hai — chahe training ho, competition ho, ya
koi bhi challenge ho.
Lekin visualization sirf trophy jeetne ke sapne dekhna nahi hai — tumhe un challenges ko
bhi imagine karna hai jo raste mein aa sakte hain, aur sochna hai ki unka samna kaise
karoge. Isse tum apni soch ko control kar sakte ho aur zyada taiyyar rahoge. Jaise main race
mein pehle course ki gaadi chala kar dekh leta hoon — success aur challenges dono ko
visualize karta hoon. Sab kuch prepare nahi kar sakte, par agar strategic visualization
karoge to zyada ready rahoge.
Aur sabse important baat, tumhe khud se simple sawal poochne honge: “Main ye kyun kar
raha hoon? Mera motivation kya hai? Mera andhera ya dard kahan se aa raha hai? Kaun si
cheezein meri soch ko tough banati hain?” Jab dard ya doubt ka wall samne aayega, tab
tumhe ye jawab apne paas hone chahiye. Tab tum apne andhere ko apna fuel banao, usse
takat lo, aur apni tough, calloused mind par bharosa karo.
Yaad rakho, visualization ka matlab ye nahi ki tum bina mehnat ke jeet jaoge. Jhoot nahi
chalta. Jo strategies main follow karta hoon, wo tabhi effective hain jab main apni mehnat karta hoon. Yeh sirf “mind over matter” nahi, yeh hai apni zindagi mein dard aur takleef ko har roz accept karna aur usse apni taqat banana.
Aur jab tum ye kar loge, to us suffering ke baad tumhari zindagi bilkul alag level par hogi —
nayi zindagi jo tumhari intezaar kar rahi hai.
Yeh challenge zaroori nahi ki physical ho, aur jeet ka matlab sirf pehla number lena bhi nahi
hota. Jeet ho sakti hai ki tumne apni zindagi ka koi bada darr ya rukawat khud par haavi
hone se roka ho.
Ab apni kahani duniya ko sunao — ki tumne apna #armoredmind kaise banaya, aur yeh
tumhe kahan le gaya.
