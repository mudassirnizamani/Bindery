CHALLENGE #4
Abhi kisi bhi competitive situation ko choose karo jisme tum ho. Tumhara opponent kaun
hai? Tumhara teacher ya coach? Tumhara boss? Koi difficult client? Chahe wo tumhare
saath kaise bhi behave kar rahe ho, ek tareeka hai unka respect jeetne ka aur situation apne
favor mein badalne ka — excellence.
Chahe exam crack karna ho, ya perfect proposal banana ho, ya sales goal smash karna ho —
jo bhi ho, main chahta hoon ki tum us project ya class mein ab tak se zyada mehnat karo. Jo
bhi wo ideal outcome expect karte hain, usse zyada achieve karne ki koshish karo.
Agar coach tumhe games mein time nahi deta, toh practice mein dominate karo. Apne
squad ke best player ko challenge karo aur apna best dikhayo. Matlab off-field bhi time do
— film dekho taaki opponent ke moves samajh sako, plays yaad karo, gym mein training
karo. Coach ko tum par dhyan dena padega.
Agar teacher hai, toh high quality work karo. Extra time assignments mein do. Aise papers
likho jo usne assign nahi kiye! Class mein jaldi aao, sawal pucho, dhyan do. Use dikhao ki
tum kaun ho aur kya banna chahte ho.
Agar boss hai, toh nonstop kaam karo. Usse pehle office jao, uske jaane ke baad niklo. Yeh
sab wo dekhein. Jab deliver karne ka time aaye, unki maximum expectations se aage badho.
Chahe jis ke saath bhi deal kar rahe ho, tumhara goal hona chahiye ki wo tumhe dekhein aur
sochein ki tumne wo achieve kiya jo wo kabhi nahi kar sake. Unhe sochna chahiye ki tum
kitne amazing ho. Unki negativity ko apne advantage mein badlo aur apni poori taakat se
unka task dominate karo. Unki motherfucking soul le lo! Baad mein social par post karo aur
hashtag #canthurtme #takingsouls laga do.
