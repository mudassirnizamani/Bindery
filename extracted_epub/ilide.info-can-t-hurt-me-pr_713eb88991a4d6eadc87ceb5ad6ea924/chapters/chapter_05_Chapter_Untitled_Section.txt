CHALLENGE #1
Mere life ke bure cards jaldi mil gaye the aur kaafi time tak saath bhi rahe, lekin life mein kisi na kisi point pe sabko challenge face karna padta hai. Tumhare life ka “bad hand” kya tha? Bachpan mein kis tarah ki dikkat jheli tumne? Kya tumhe maara gaya? Abuse hua? Bully kiya gaya? Kya tum kabhi insecure feel karte the?
Ya ho sakta hai tumhara limiting factor yeh ho ki tum itne zyada support aur comfort mein bade hue ki kabhi khud ko push hi nahi kiya?
Aaj ke time mein kya cheezein tumhari growth aur success ko rok rahi hain? Kya koi tumhare raaste mein khada hai—workplace ya school mein? Kya tumhe value nahi mil rahi? Opportunities milke bhi ignore kiya jaa raha hai? Ya kahin aisa toh nahi ki tum khud hi apne raste ka blockage ho?
Apna ek journal nikalo—agar nahi hai toh ek kharid lo, ya laptop, tablet ya phone ke notes app mein likhna shuru karo—and sab kuch detail mein likho. Bland mat likhna. Main tumhe apna sab ganda sach dikhaya hai. Agar tumhe kabhi dard hua hai ya abhi bhi ho raha hai, toh usse poori honesty ke saath likho. Apne pain ko shape do. Uski power ko absorb karo, kyunki ab tum us pain ko flip karne wale ho.
Apni story, apne excuses ka list—jo bhi wajah hai jinki wajah se lagta hai ki tum kuch nahi ban sakte—isi ko apni success ka fuel banaoge.
Mazaa aayega na? Nahi, aisa kuch nahi hone wala. Lekin abhi uski tension mat lo. Pehle toh bas apni list banao.
Jab list ban jaaye, toh use jis tarah chaaho share karo. Kuch log social media pe post karke apne emotions share karte hain—ek photo ke saath kuch lines likh ke batate hain ki kaise unka past ya present unhe andar tak challenge karta hai. Agar tum waise ho, toh hashtags use karo:
#badhand #canthurtme
Nahi toh privately accept karo. Jo bhi tumhare liye work kare.
Mujhe pata hai yeh step tough hai, lekin sirf yeh step uthana hi tumhe strong feel karne lagega.
