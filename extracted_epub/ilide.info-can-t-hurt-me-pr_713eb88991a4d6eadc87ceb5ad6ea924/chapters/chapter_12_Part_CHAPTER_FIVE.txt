mein dekha. Do saal se main extreme pain le raha tha aur har baar wapas aake aur aage
badhne ki koshish kar raha tha. Kabhi kabhi jeet milti thi, par failure mein bhi dab jata tha.
Us raat, mujhe aage badhne ki sirf ek wajah mili — wo thi ki jitna bhi dard jhela, usne meri
mind ko callous kiya tha. Sawal ye tha ki callous kitna mota hai? Kitna dard ek aadmi le
sakta hai? Kya mere andar tootey huye pairon ke saath daudne ki himmat hai?
Main agle din 3:30 baje utha, base chala gaya. BUD/S ke cage tak limp karta hua gaya, jahan
hum apna gear rakhte the, aur ek bench par baith gaya, apna backpack apne pairon ke paas
giraya. Andhera tha andar bhi, bahar bhi, aur main akela tha. Door se samundar ki lehron ki
awaaz aa rahi thi. Main apne dive bag mein haath dala, jisme do rolls duct tape chhupe the.
Maine socha ki mera plan kitna paagal hai, par main muskuraya aur tape uthaya.
Main apne right foot par ek mota black tube sock dhala. Mera shin bahut sensitive tha,
thoda bhi ankle ka hilna dard badha deta tha. Maine tape ko apne heel ke around loop kiya,
phir ankle ke upar aur wapas neeche, phir dono feet aur calf ko tightly wrap kar diya. Ye
pehla layer tha. Phir ek aur black tube sock pehna aur wapas tape laga. Jab main finish kar
raha tha, toh meri dono lower legs aur feet dono socks aur tape ki layers mein lapet chuke
the, jaise soft cast ho. Boot pehen ke, mera ankle aur shin immobilized ho gaya tha. Phir left
foot bhi waise hi kiya.
Ek ghante baad, dono lower legs mujhe soft casts mein dalne jaisi lag rahi thi. Chalna abhi
bhi dard deta tha, par jab ankle move hota tha toh jo torture hota tha wo thoda kam ho gaya
tha. Ya kam se kam main ye soch raha tha. Jab running start karenge tab pata chalega.
Pehli training run meri aazmaish thi, aur main poori koshish kar raha tha ki apne hip flexors
se daudoon. Usually ham apne pairo aur lower legs se rhythm banate hain, par mujhe usse
ulta karna tha. Mujhe har movement ko dhyan se isolate karna tha, taaki hip se neeche ki
power generate kar saku. Pehle 30 minute mein dard apni zindagi ka sabse zyada dard tha.
Tape meri skin ko kata, aur running ke har step par mere fractures mein shokwaves dard
uthane lage.
Aur ye sirf pehla run tha. Agle paanch mahine har din ye dard jhelna tha. Kya main survive
kar paunga? Maine socha quit karne ka bhi. Agar failure mera bhavishya hai aur mujhe apni
zindagi badalni padegi, to phir ye sab karne ka kya matlab hai? Kyun main is sab ko delay
kar raha hoon? Kya main pagal hoon? Har ek soch ek hi simple sawal par aake rukti thi:
“kyun?”
“Guarantee failure ka sirf ek hi tareeka hai, aur wo hai abhi quit kar dena, motherfucker!”
Main apne aap se chillata tha, apne dard aur bechaini ke shor ke beech chupke se cheekhta
tha. “Dard ko jhel, warna ye sirf tera failure nahi hoga, tera parivaar ka bhi failure hoga!”
Main soch raha tha ki agar main sach mein ye sab kar paoon, ye mission pura karoon, to
kaisa feel hoga. Agar main wo dard jhel saku jo is mission ko pura karne ke liye zaroori hai.
Ye soch mujhe ek aadha mile aur aage daudne ki taqat de gayi, phir wo dard phir se ek
tufaan ki tarah mere andar ghoomne laga.
“Zyada log healthy hokar bhi BUD/S se guzarte hain, aur tum tootey pairon ke saath ja rahe
ho! Aur kaun sochta hai aisa?” maine apne aap se kaha.
“Kaun ek toote pair par ek minute tak bhi daud payega, do pairon par to chhodo? Sirf
Goggins! Tum abhi 20 minute ho chuke ho is business mein, Goggins! Tum ek machine ho!
Har kadam jo tum ab tak chaloge, tumhe aur zyada tough banayega!”
Wo baat mere liye ek password crack karne jaisi thi. Mera calloused mind mera aage
badhne ka ticket tha, aur 40 minute ke baad ek ajeeb badlav aaya. Dard kam ho gaya. Tape
thoda loose ho gaya tha, isliye wo meri skin ko kat nahi raha tha, aur meri muscles aur
haddiyan itni garam ho gayi thi ki thodi maar jhel sakti thi. Dard din bhar aata jaata raha,
par manageable ho gaya, aur jab bhi dard aata, main khud ko kehta ki ye mere tough hone
ka proof hai, aur main aur bhi zyada tough hota ja raha hoon.
Din-ba-din yehi ritual repeat hota raha. Main jaldi base par aata, apne pairo ko duct tape
lagata, 30 minute tak extreme dard jhelta, apne aap ko motivate karta, aur survive karta. Ye
koi fake-it-till-you-make-it nahi tha. Mere liye ye baat hi amazing thi ki main har din itna
kuch karne ke liye ready tha. Instructors ne bhi meri koshish ki kadar ki. Unhone kaha ki wo
mere haath aur pairo ko bandh kar mujhe pool mein daalenge taaki main chaar lap tair saku.
Ye evolution unka Drown Proofing hota tha, jise main controlled drowning kehta hoon!
Haath piche bandh ke, pairo ko bhi baandh kar, hum sirf dolphin kick kar sakte the. Kuch
experienced swimmers aise the jaise Michael Phelps ke genes lekar aaye ho, par meri
dolphin kick ek rocking horse jaisi thi, jo zyada propulsion nahi deti thi. Main hamesha
saans lene ke liye apna sir paani ke upar uthata, phir doob jata, zor se kick lagata,
momentum paane ki koshish karta, lekin asar kam milta. Maine iske liye kafi practice ki thi.
Hafte bhar main pool mein practice karta raha, wetsuit shorts pehne ki koshish ki taaki
uniform ke neeche kuch buoyancy mile. Wo shorts mujhe diaper jaisa dikhate the, aur koi
madad nahi karte the, par practice se main drowning ki feeling se itna comfortable ho gaya
ki test pass kar saku.
Second Phase mein, jise dive phase kehte hain, ek aur bhayanak underwater evolution tha.
Ismein wapas hume paani mein tairna hota tha, lekin is baar hum full charged, twin
eighty-liter tanks aur 16-pound weight belt ke saath hote. Fins hote the, par unke saath kicking se mere ankles aur shins ko zyada dard hota tha. Main tape nahi laga sakta tha paani ke andar, to mujhe dard jhelna padta tha.
Uske baad hume apni peeth par tairna tha 50 meter bina doobte, phir palat kar apne pet par
tairna tha 50 meter, bina doobte, wo bhi full gear ke saath! Koi flotation device allowed nahi
tha. Sirf apna sir upar rakhna bahut dard deta tha — gardan, kandhe, hips, aur lower back
sab mein.
Paani mein jo awaazein nikalti thi wo main kabhi nahi bhool sakta. Humari zinda rehne ki
larai ki awaazein — gurgling, grunting, gasping — thi. Kabhi kabhi kuch log doob jate, apna
weight belt nikal dete, tanks chod dete, aur surface par aa jate. Sirf ek hi aadmi pehli
koshish mein pass hua tha is evolution ko. Hamare paas teen koshish thi kisi bhi evolution
ko pass karne ki, aur mujhe teenon koshish karni padi.
Akhri koshish mein main lambi, smooth scissor kicks par focus kiya, apne overworked hip
flexors se. Mushkil se pass hua tha.
Jab Third Phase, yani land warfare training module San Clemente Island par aayi, tab mere
pair theek ho chuke the. Mujhe pata tha ki graduation tak main survive kar lunga, par iska
matlab ye nahi tha ki wo aasan hoga. BUD/S ke main compound, The Strand par, bahut log
dekhne aate hain — officers aate hain, jo instructors ke kandhon ke upar dekhte hain. Lekin
island par sirf tum aur instructors hote ho, wo bindaas ho jate hain, aur koi daya nahi
dikhate. Isi wajah se mujhe island pasand tha!
Ek din dopahar ko hum do-do aur teen-teen logon ki teams mein bante hue chhupne ke
liye jagah banane lage jo ped-paudhon mein ache se ghul mil jayein. Tab tak training ka end
nazdeek tha, sab log zabardast shape mein the aur dare nahi rahe the. Lekin thodi der baad
kuch log apna dhyan kam dene lage, jisse instructors naraaz ho gaye. To unhone hume ek ghaati mein bulaaya aur classic beat down diya — push-ups, sit-ups, flutter kicks, aur eight-count bodybuilders (advanced burpees) itne ki bharmaar thi.
Par sabse pehle hume ghutne ke bal baith kar apne haathon se itne bade gaddhe khodne
pade ki hum apna sar gaddhe ke bahar nikal kar daba saken, ek anjaane samay ke liye. Main
khush ho kar gehera gaddha khod raha tha, tabhi ek instructor ne mujhe torture karne ka
naya tareeka nikala.
“Goggins, uth ja. Tumhe ye sab maza aa raha hai.” Main hansa aur khodta raha, lekin wo
serious tha.
“Maine kaha uth ja, Goggins. Tum zyada maza le rahe ho.”
Main khada ho gaya, side mein chala gaya, aur apne classmates ko agle tees minute tak dard
jhelte dekha, bina mere. Tab se instructors mujhe apne beat downs mein shamil karna band
kar diye. Jab class ko push-ups, sit-ups karne ya paani aur rait mein girne ka hukm milta, to
mujhe hamesha exclude kar dete.
Main ise apni jeet samajhta tha — ki main poore BUD/S staff ki himmat tod chuka tha. Lekin
sach kahun to mujhe wo beat downs ki kami mehsoos hui. Kyunki main unhe apni mind ko
callous karne ka moka samajhta tha. Ab wo mera part nahi rahe.
Grinder training ke liye center stage tha, to BUD/S graduation bhi wahi hoti hai. Parivaar
waale aate hain — fathers aur brothers apni chhaati pher kar khade hote, mothers, wives,
girlfriends sab sundar tayar. Dard aur museebat ki jagah khushi ke chehre hote us patch of
asphalt par jab Class 235 ke graduates apne safed dress mein ek bade American flag ke
neeche khade hote.
Hamare right mein wo infamous bell thi jise 130 classmates ne tola tha, jinhe apni training
chhodni padi — duniya ki sabse challenging military training. Har ek ka naam bulaya gaya aur individually acknowledge kiya gaya. Meri maa ki aankhon mein khushi ke aansu the jab mera naam aaya, par mujhe ajeeb sa dukh hi mehsoos hua.
Grinder aur baad mein McP’s, jo downtown Coronado ka SEALs ka pasandida pub hai,
wahan mere teammates apne parivaar ke saath khush hote photo le rahe the. Bar mein
music tez tha, sab nashe mein apna jashn mana rahe the, jaise koi badi jeet ho gayi ho. Par
sach kahun, to mujhe ye sab annoy karta tha, kyunki mujhe BUD/S chhodna pasand nahi
tha.
Jab main pehli baar SEALs mein interested tha, to main aise maidan ki talaash mein tha jo
mujhe ya to pura tod de ya mujhe todne se rok de — unbreakable bana de. BUD/S ne ye
dikhaya ki insan ka dimaag kitna powerful hota hai, aur dard jhel kar kaise apne aap ko itna
majboot bana sakta hai ki wo wo sab kar sake jo kabhi socha bhi na ho, jaise toote pairon
par daudna.
Graduation ke baad sab kuch mere haath mein tha ki main impossible tasks ki talaash jari
rakhun. Kyunki chahe main Navy SEALs history ka sirf teesra African American BUD/S
graduate tha, meri asli journey, odds ko harane ki quest, abhi shuru hi hui thi!
