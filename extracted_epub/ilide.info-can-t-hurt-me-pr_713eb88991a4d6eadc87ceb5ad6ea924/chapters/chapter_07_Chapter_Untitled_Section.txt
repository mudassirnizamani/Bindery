CHALLENGE #2
Ab waqt aa gaya hai khud se aankhon mein aankhon daal kar baat karne ka — bilkul raw aur real tareeke se. Ye koi self-love exercise nahi hai. Isme tum apne ego ko maska nahi maar sakte. Ye ego ko todne ka process hai — aur asli tum tak pahuchne ka pehla step.
Main ne apne
Accountability Mirror
pe Post-It notes chipkaye the, aur ab main tumse bhi wahi expect karta hoon. Phone ya laptop pe likhne se kaam nahi chalega. Apni sari insecurities, dreams, aur goals ko Post-Its pe likho aur mirror pe chipka do.
Agar tumhe aur padhna hai, toh likho — “Padhaai mein weak hoon, isiliye mehnat karni padegi!” Simple. Agar mirror mein ek overweight insaan dikh raha hai, toh likho — “Main fat hoon!” Accept karo. Aise waqt mein thoda harsh hona theek hai — life mein grow karne ke liye skin thick honi chahiye.
Chahe goal career ka ho (jaise job chhod kar business start karna), lifestyle ka ho (weight lose karna, zyada active hona), ya fitness ka ho (apna pehla 5K, 10K ya marathon run karna), tumhe khud ke current reality ke baare mein brutally honest hona hoga. Har goal ke liye daily step likho. Har ek chhoti improvement ek alag Post-It note honi chahiye.
Example ke liye — agar tumhe 40 pounds weight lose karna hai, toh pehla note ho sakta hai: “Pehle week mein 2 pounds lose karna hai.” Jab wo achieve ho jaaye, us note ko hata do aur naya goal chipkao: “Next 2–5 pounds lose karna.” Aise karte karte final goal tak pahuchna hai.
Jo bhi tumhara goal ho, uske chhote-chhote steps ke liye khud ko accountable banao. Self-improvement sirf dedication aur discipline se hota hai.
Wo dirty mirror jo tum daily dekhte ho, wo hamesha tumhe sach batata hai — usse ignore karna band karo. Usse apna tool banao.
